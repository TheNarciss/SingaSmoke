# SingaSmoke privacy policy

*Effective 10 October 2026.*

SingaSmoke shows where smoking is allowed and banned in Singapore, and where the licensed tobacco retailers are, from public data. It is built to collect nothing about you.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no advertising identifier.
- Nothing about you is sent to the developer of SingaSmoke, and the developer runs no server.

## Your location

The app asks for your location **only while it is open** ("When In Use"), never in the background. It uses it **on your iPhone** to tell you whether you are in a no-smoking zone, to show the way out of one, and to list the nearest smoking areas and licensed shops. Your location is not stored, and it is not sent to the developer.

You can refuse or withdraw location access at any time in Settings. The app then works from the centre of the map instead.

## Services the app relies on

Some features call services run by others. They receive what any app or website request reveals, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies:

- **Apple Maps (MapKit):** the map itself, walking routes and times, and Apple Maps place cards with shop opening hours. To compute a route, Apple receives the start and end points of the route. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **National Environment Agency (NEA):** official photos of designated smoking areas are loaded from NEA's servers when you open one.
- **Google Maps:** only if you tap the "Google Maps" button, which hands the destination to Google's app or website.

## Data in the app

The smoking areas, no-smoking zones and licensed retailers come from public open data: NEA, NParks and HSA datasets on data.gov.sg (Singapore Open Data Licence), the Changi Airport Group website, and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). This data is bundled in the app and updated with new versions.

## Children

SingaSmoke is rated 18+. In Singapore, tobacco may only be bought and smoked from the age of 21.
