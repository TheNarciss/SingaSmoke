Can I smoke here? SingaSmoke answers in seconds, anywhere in Singapore, and shows the way to the nearest place where smoking is allowed.

Smoking is banned in many public places in Singapore, and fines start at S$200. The rules are clear, but on the street it is hard to tell where a no-smoking zone ends. SingaSmoke puts the official data on one map.

WHERE YOU STAND
• A status bar at the top of the map tells you, from your location, whether you are in a known no-smoking place: the Orchard Road No-Smoking Zone, parks and gardens, beaches, reservoirs, bus stops, playgrounds, fitness corners, sports courts, schools, hospitals, hawker centres, bus interchanges and multi-storey car parks.
• Inside a zone, it shows the shortest way out.
• Close to the edge of a zone, it says so: GPS is not precise enough to be sure.

WHERE SMOKING IS ALLOWED
• NEA's designated smoking areas, the yellow boxes of Orchard Road, with NEA's photo of each.
• The smoking areas of Changi Airport.
• Smoking corners mapped by OpenStreetMap contributors, marked as indicative.
• Sorted by real walking time, with walking directions in the app, or in Apple Maps and Google Maps.

LICENSED SHOPS
• The Health Sciences Authority's public register of licensed tobacco retailers, by kind of shop, with opening hours where they are known.

HOW FAR TO TRUST IT
Every place shows its source: official (NEA, NParks, HSA, Changi Airport Group) or indicative (OpenStreetMap). No map shows building interiors, covered walkways, lift lobbies, void decks or the 5 m around entrances, where smoking is banned too. Always go by the signs on site.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used on your iPhone, only while the app is open, and never sent to the developer.

SingaSmoke is an independent app, not affiliated with NEA, NParks, HSA, SLA or Changi Airport Group. In Singapore, tobacco is for adults aged 21 and over, and vapes and e-cigarettes are illegal, even to possess.
